Before You Start

Prepare the required documents before starting your TradeGP application.

Documents Required

  • ✓ MyKad (Front) / Passport
  • ✓ MyKad (Back) / Passport
  • ✓ Selfie holding your MyKad / Passport
  • ✓ Latest bank statement

Upload Requirements

  • ✓ PDF or JPEG format
  • ✓ Maximum file size: 5 MB per document
  • ✓ Documents must be clear and readable
  • ✓ Remove password protection where applicable
Tip: Prepare all required documents before completing your registration to avoid unnecessary delays.

Document Upload Guide

Check the examples below before uploading your identification document.

MyKad / Passport

Compare the examples below to make sure your document is clear and complete.

✕ Not Acceptable

Blurry

Document details are unclear.

✕ Not Acceptable

Glare

Light reflection covers important details.

✕ Not Acceptable

Edges Not Fully Visible

Make sure all four document edges are fully visible.

✓ Acceptable

Clear & Acceptable

Clear, fully visible and free from glare or blur.

Bank Statement

  • Do not convert screenshots into PDF.
  • Remove password protection before uploading.
  • Bank logo must be visible.
  • Your full name must be displayed.
  • Bank account number must be visible.
  • Include the latest one-month transaction(s).

Selfie Photo

Take a clear selfie while holding your MyKad or passport. Make sure your face and identification document are fully visible.

Avoid glare, blur and cropped document edges.

How to Get Started

Follow the steps below to complete your TradeGP onboarding process.

Sign Up Application Review Account Activation Security Setup Complete
STEP 1

Begin Your Registration

Go to the TradeGP website and click Sign Up to begin your registration.

View detailed steps
Detailed Steps
  1. Go to the TradeGP website.
  2. Click Sign Up.
  3. Enter the required registration information.
  4. Complete the verification process.
STEP 2

Confirm Your Email Address

Open the verification email and click the activation link to continue your application.

View detailed steps
Detailed Steps
  1. Open your registered email inbox.
  2. Find the TradeGP verification email.
  3. Click the activation link in the email.
STEP 3

Complete Your Registration Form

Choose your subscription plan and complete all required sections of the application form.

View detailed steps
Information Required
  • Personal information
  • Employment information
  • Financial information
  • Declarations
  • Required supporting documents
STEP 4

Application Review & Approval

After submitting your application, you will receive an Application Submitted Successfully email. Once approved, you will receive an email titled Your Account Application is Approved!

STEP 5

Activate Your Trading Account

Once your application is approved, open the email titled Your Account Application is Approved! and follow the instructions to activate your trading account.

View detailed steps
Activate Your Trading Account
  1. Open the email titled Your Account Application is Approved!
  2. Open the attached account opening document.
  3. Review your account details.
  4. Click ACTIVATE TRADING ACCOUNT .
  5. Continue with the trading account setup process.
STEP 6

Create Login Credentials

Create your TradeGP username, password and trading PIN to secure your account.

View detailed steps
Create Your Login Information
  1. Create your TradeGP username.
  2. Create and confirm your password.
  3. Create your Trading PIN.
  4. Review the information before continuing.
STEP 7

Set Up Google Authenticator

Scan the QR code using Google Authenticator and enter the generated 6-digit verification code.

View detailed steps
Detailed Steps
  1. Open Google Authenticator.
  2. Tap the + button.
  3. Select Scan a QR code.
  4. Scan the QR code displayed on the TradeGP page.
  5. Enter the generated 6-digit verification code.
  6. Continue to complete the activation.
STEP 8

Account Activated SUCCESS

Your trading account has been activated successfully.

Next: Continue to complete your TradeGP setup.
STEP 9

TradeGP Setup Complete COMPLETE

Your TradeGP account setup is complete.

You're ready: You can now proceed to the TradeGP App.
